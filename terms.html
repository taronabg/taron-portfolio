Terms of service

1. Agreement

TA² Websites (“TA²”, “we”, “us”) designs, builds, hosts and maintains websites. Each project is covered by a written agreement. If anything in these terms conflicts with your signed agreement, the signed agreement wins.

2. Free mockup

We may prepare a website mockup before you commit. Seeing the mockup is free and creates no obligation. Until you sign and pay a deposit, the mockup design remains our property.

3. Payment

  • Builds are paid 50% up front to start and 50% when the website goes live.
  • Hosting, maintenance and updates are billed under a yearly plan that renews on the anniversary of your signing date unless cancelled.
  • Domains are registered in your business’s name unless agreed otherwise in writing.

4. What the yearly plan covers

Hosting, security updates, backups and small content updates each month, as described in your agreement. Larger changes, such as new pages or features, are quoted separately before any work starts.

5. Ownership

  • Your business name, logo, photos, written content and domain always belong to you.
  • While you’re on a yearly plan, the website design and code are licensed to you for your business and hosted by us.
  • You can buy out your website at any time for the buyout fee in your agreement. After the buyout, we transfer the website files, hosting and logins to you, and the website is fully yours.
  • If a yearly plan ends without a buyout, hosting ends. Your content and domain stay yours.

6. Portfolio

With your permission, we may show your website in our portfolio and marketing.

7. Limitation of liability

We build every website to bring in business, but we can’t guarantee specific results such as rankings, calls or revenue. We aren’t liable for indirect losses or lost profits.

Privacy policy

What we collect

When you use a form on this site, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number and message. When you book a call, Calendly collects your booking details.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and discuss your project
  • To send quotes, project updates and invoices
  • To follow up about work you’ve asked us about

Services we use

Form messages are delivered through Formspree. Bookings run through Calendly. This site uses Google tags to measure visits and ad performance, which may set cookies in your browser. We don’t sell or trade your information.
